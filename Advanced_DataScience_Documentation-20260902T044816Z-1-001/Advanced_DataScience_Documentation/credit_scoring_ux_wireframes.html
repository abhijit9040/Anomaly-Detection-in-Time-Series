Intended use
This model is intended to assist qualified credit risk professionals as a decision-support tool only. It must not be used as the sole basis for credit approval, rejection, or pricing. Output should be reviewed in combination with human judgement, additional data sources, and regulatory compliance checks.
Limitations
Known constraints and out-of-scope uses
Known limitations
US-centric training dataDataset reflects US consumer credit patterns from 2005–2010. Performance on other geographies or more recent lending cycles is not validated.
Class imbalanceThe dataset is 93.3% non-default. Model may underperform on minority-class edge cases despite SMOTE balancing during training.
Missing income handling~20% of training records had missing MonthlyIncome. Median imputation was applied — predictions for low-income or variable-income applicants carry higher uncertainty.
No behavioural or bureau dataModel does not incorporate credit bureau scores, payment history trends, or behavioural signals. These factors are typically material to default prediction.
Static thresholdThe 0.50 decision threshold is fixed. It may need recalibration as the lending portfolio or economic conditions change.
Out of scope
Automated credit decisions without human review
Use in regulated ECOA / fair lending contexts without bias audit
Real-time production scoring at scale without monitoring
Business, SME, or non-consumer lending applications
